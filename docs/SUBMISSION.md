# Pendiente submission

Pendiente lets someone abroad buy a meal for a person back home. The stall is paid only when the plate is served. A buyer approves digital dollars on Stellar, then shares a paper card. The stall scans it once. The buyer sees the plate photo. If nobody uses the card before it expires, the money returns.

## How it works

1. Pick a stall and approve its listed price.
2. Send the card by WhatsApp or print it.
3. Scan it at the named stall.
4. The stall is paid and the meal page updates.

Stellar keeps the fee under one cent, settles in about 5 seconds, and gives anyone a public record. The token used here is testnet USDC play money. Stalls can later use an anchor for cash-out; that is future work.

## 60-second judge walkthrough

Open the live site, choose the listed stall, connect a testnet wallet, approve the price, and show the card. Open the counter link on another phone, scan the card, serve it, and show the meal page with its payment link and photo. Scan the same card again to show the refusal.
